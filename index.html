Prem Predict

A Premier League match-forecasting product, from raw data to a live site.

Ingests 33 seasons of match and player data, builds leakage-safe rolling pre-match features, and serves calibrated home, draw, and away probabilities. The production model is Dixon-Coles with ridge-shrunk team ratings and time decay, and an XGBoost blend is only promoted when a gameweek-block bootstrap confidence interval says it actually wins. Users can sign in and save lineup scenarios to re-run against the current model.

MAGI: Incident Council

Three AI agents with competing mandates negotiate a live production incident.

One agent restores service, one contains harm, one protects customers, and each sees only the evidence it's entitled to. They propose blind, rank options with a Schulze vote, critique and amend, and act alone only on reversible steps they agree on. When they genuinely disagree about something irreversible, MAGI phones a human with the crux. I built an evaluation harness on 13 decision points adapted from real postmortems (Cloudflare, GitLab, AWS S3, Knight Capital) and reported the results honestly, including where the council lost to simpler baselines.
